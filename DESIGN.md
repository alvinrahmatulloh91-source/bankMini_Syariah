# DESIGN.md — LKM Mitra Siswa Abadi

## Brand Identity

Project: LKM Mitra Siswa Abadi — Bank Mini Perbankan Syariah SMKN 1 Bantul.

Design character:
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Fresh
- Warm
- Educational
- Contemporary
- Human-centered

The website should feel like a real institutional banking service, not a generic fintech landing page.

## Color Direction

Primary green: #16834A
Dark green: #105C38
Deep green: #0B3D2E
Soft green: #EAF5EE
Light background: #F8FAF8

Accent orange: #F28C28
Dark orange: #D96F12
Soft orange: #FFF2E5

Text primary: #17251D
Text secondary: #64746A
Border: #E5EBE6
White: #FFFFFF

Green is the dominant brand color. Orange is reserved for meaningful emphasis and selected calls to action.

## Typography

Use Plus Jakarta Sans or Inter.

Use a clear hierarchy:
- Strong but controlled page headings.
- Readable body text.
- Consistent font weights.
- Comfortable line height.
- Restrained use of uppercase text.

## Layout Direction

Use an editorial institutional website layout.

Vary section composition:
- Split hero.
- Editorial about section.
- Historical timeline.
- Structured service listing.
- Information-rich service details.
- Location and contact layout.
- FAQ accordion.

Do not force every section into identical cards.

## Imagery

Prioritize authentic photographs of:
- Bank Mini interior.
- Student banking practice.
- Teller and customer service activities.
- School environment.
- Official institutional identity.

Never present generated images as actual institutional documentation.

Use clear placeholders until authentic assets are available.

## Motion Direction

Motion should feel subtle, intentional, and responsive.

Use:
- Fade and translate entrance.
- Staggered content reveal.
- Subtle button feedback.
- Navigation transitions.
- Timeline reveal.
- Accessible accordion transitions.

Avoid excessive parallax, continuous floating elements, unnecessary glow, and decorative animation without purpose.

Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

## Content Direction

Use factual, informative, concise bilingual content.

Avoid:
- Fake statistics.
- Fake testimonials.
- Unsupported marketing claims.
- Generic AI-generated slogans.
- Repetitive descriptions.

Preserve the official identity, service details, and terminology of LKM Mitra Siswa Abadi.

## Responsive Direction

Design mobile and desktop as intentional layouts.

Ensure readable text, appropriate spacing, usable navigation, accessible buttons, and no horizontal overflow.

## Overall Design Goal

Create a distinctive, authentic, polished institutional website with a green-and-orange identity, meaningful content, and restrained motion.

The result must feel deliberately designed for LKM Mitra Siswa Abadi rather than assembled from a generic website template.
